# MicMon

MicMon is a macOS app for checking your Mac's microphone input in real time.

Pick a microphone from the connected input devices, and MicMon shows per-channel level meters (peak and RMS), a scrolling waveform of the last few seconds, a frequency spectrum, and a spectrogram at a glance. Clipping is easy to spot, and from within the app you can listen to the input through your speakers or headphones and adjust the system input volume.

Use it in a regular window, or switch to a small card that stays on top of other windows while you work. It's handy for a quick check that your mic is picking up sound, and not too loudly, before a call or a recording.
